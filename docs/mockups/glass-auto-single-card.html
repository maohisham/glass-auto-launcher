Glass Auto: one card — mock-up

Hand-drawn to show the layout, not a screenshot from the car. The picture is drawn by the app at the same proportions as the real one. Maps stays on the main area.

What may differ in the car. Android Auto decides how big the picture is and how it crops it. I drew it square. If the car crops the edges, the right-hand times could be clipped.

Not guaranteed. Staying on this card when the music app changes track depends on Android Auto, and I can't confirm it from here.
