Glass Auto cards — mock-up

Drawn by hand to show the layout, not screenshots from the car. Android Auto keeps Maps on the main area and shows one media card beside it. You switch between the cards from the media list.

What this does not show. Cards can't sit side by side. Only one is on screen at a time, and you pick it from the media list. Playing a song or a station from the Glass cards isn't offered: you start music in YouTube Music or VRadio, and Lyrics follows it.
